Excel Functions & Modeling Architecture

Documentation of the 7 core Excel functions and dynamic worksheet features engineered inside EMI Calculator.xlsx.

PMT() Financial Function

Calculates the constant periodic loan payment based on a constant interest rate and payment schedule.

=PMT(rate/freq, tenure*freq, -(loan - downpayment))
PPMT() Principal Component

Returns the principal portion of a specific installment period, increasing steadily over the loan's life.

=PPMT(rate/freq, period_no, total_periods, -net_loan)
IPMT() Interest Component

Calculates the interest portion for a given installment period based on the reducing principal balance.

=IPMT(rate/freq, period_no, total_periods, -net_loan)
VLOOKUP() Lookup & Reference

Dynamically fetches the number of installments per year from the Raw Information tab when the user changes the EMI Frequency dropdown.

=VLOOKUP(C10, 'Raw Information '!$B$5:$C$12, 2, FALSE)
EDATE() Date & Time

Advances installment dates cleanly by exact calendar months for Monthly, BI-Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, and Annual frequencies.

=EDATE(prev_date, 12 / installments_per_year)
ROUND() Math & Precision

Computes exact day intervals (ROUND(365 / freq, 0)) for sub-monthly schedules (Weekly, Fortnightly, Every 15 days).

=prev_date + ROUND(365 / installments_per_year, 0)
IF() $A$1 Locking
Logical Control & Modeling

Wraps schedule rows to automatically suppress rows beyond the active installment count (IF(row > total_installments, "", ...)), paired with Data Validation dropdowns and mixed/absolute cell locking ($) for the What-If EMI sensitivity matrix.
